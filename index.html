Our Story

Built to Close the Gap

Canada has one of the most persistent skills gap problems in the developed world. Hundreds of thousands of jobs sit unfilled — not because people aren't willing to work, but because the training pathways are fragmented, expensive, or disconnected from what employers actually need.

SkillBridges was built to change that. By combining high-quality video production, real industry instructors, and curriculum that's directly tied to labour market data, we've created a platform where learning leads somewhere — to a real job, in a real field, in Canada.
